# Physical Safari handoff — not yet tested

Use a small iPhone, a modern notched/Dynamic Island iPhone, and an iPad. Record model, iOS/iPadOS version, Safari version, orientation, delivery URL/build commit and pass/fail evidence. Linux automation cannot complete these checks.

1. Open the complete hosted build in Safari. Expand/collapse the browser toolbars, rotate both ways and pinch zoom. Check the notch/home indicator, Return, answers and Next. On iPad repeat portrait, landscape and narrow Split View/Stage Manager widths.
2. In the hero creator change both character styles, outfit and equipment; edit the name with the real keyboard. Scroll all options, dismiss the keyboard and finish. Confirm no unwanted input autozoom, covered fields or unreachable action buttons.
3. In Librarian answer incorrectly. Hold Right with one thumb and jump with another; hold Duck then release; use Dash and Shield. Hold the same direction with two fingers then lift one. Drag outside a held button, rotate while holding, switch apps and return. Movement must stop after interrupted/released touches, with visible high/low warnings and hero.
4. During a question and again during a book storm, switch apps, lock/unlock and return. Confirm no unexpected extra grading, stuck movement or duplicated animations. Close/reopen, reload, and confirm hero, earned seals, learning history and sound settings persist. Temporary interrupted duels may restart according to the existing save contract.
5. Play a guardian encounter, a Lab mistake/repair, Quick Recall and Written Practice. Verify keyboard submission, explanations, Next, settings and dialog scrolling. Enable Reduce Motion and repeat feedback/navigation. Check VoiceOver focus when opening/closing Librarian and native dialogs.
6. With the game already loaded, enable Airplane Mode and play/grade/save, then restore network and reload to verify the save. Separately test the complete standalone HTML through the intended local delivery workflow; Files/Quick Look is not assumed to execute it as a website. A Home Screen bookmark is not evidence of installable/offline PWA support.
7. Observe a detailed winged hero, animated guardian and active book storm for 60 seconds each, then reopen each 10 times. Note delayed taps, heat, frame stalls or Safari reloads. Desktop frame timings do not establish device performance or battery life.

Any OS interruption, real keyboard/toolbar, safe-area, local-file or device performance result remains **untested** until someone records it here.
